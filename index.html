Privacy Policy for Purple Penguin: Sky Bound

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Dark Matter Studios ("we," "us," or "our") provides the mobile game Purple Penguin: Sky Bound.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, collected, used, shared, retained, and protected when you use Purple Penguin: Sky Bound.

Information and Data

Purple Penguin: Sky Bound does not require users to create a Dark Matter Studios account.

The game uses third-party services and software development kits provided by Google to support advertising, Google Play Games features, leaderboards, achievements, and in-app purchases.

These services may automatically collect or process certain information when you use the game. Depending on the service and your device settings, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Device and account identifiers, including advertising-related identifiers;
  • App and user interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions;
  • Diagnostic and performance information;
  • Google Play Games information associated with achievements, leaderboards, and player functionality; and
  • Information necessary to process, verify, and restore purchases made through Google Play.

Dark Matter Studios does not sell users' personal or sensitive information.

Advertising

Purple Penguin: Sky Bound uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share information such as IP address, user interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for purposes including advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Advertising identifiers and the availability of personalized advertising may depend on your device settings, applicable law, consent choices, and Google's advertising services and policies.

Google Play Games

Purple Penguin: Sky Bound uses Google Play Games Services for features including player sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, and related game functionality.

Information associated with Google Play Games is processed by Google according to Google's applicable terms, privacy policies, and user settings.

In-App Purchases

Purple Penguin: Sky Bound offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

Purchase transactions are processed by Google Play. Dark Matter Studios does not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information.

Information relating to purchases may be processed as necessary to verify purchases, provide purchased content, and restore eligible purchases.

Information processed through Purple Penguin: Sky Bound and its third-party services may be used to:
